Why do the roots select exponential solutions?

Differentiating y = erx gives y′ = rerx and y″ = r²erx. For a constant-coefficient homogeneous equation,

ay″ + by′ + cy = (ar² + br + c)erx = p(r)erx.

Since erx is never zero, the trial function satisfies the equation for every x exactly when p(r) = 0. This is the characteristic equation. The equation chooses the allowed values of r; initial conditions choose the coefficients in a combination of independent solutions.

The display samples −1 ≤ x ≤ 1. The “Matches for every x” result follows from the exact polynomial test, rather than a visual match at a few plotted points.
