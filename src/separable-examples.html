Read the field before separating variables

The differential equation gives the slope at each valid point. An initial condition selects a curve through one point. When separating variables, check any values excluded by division, and keep the original equation’s domain in view.

For the square-root equation, y = ±1 are constant solutions lost when dividing by √(1 − y²). For the implicit example, y = 1 is excluded from the differential equation itself. These are different situations.
